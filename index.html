What am I currently working on?

01

Distributed Collaboration
in Mixed Reality

How do people collaborate when they share digital objects across physical and virtual spaces? We design and evaluate XR systems that support new forms of co-presence, shared attention, and joint action.

Human-Centred AI &

Building AI systems that adapt to users, explain their reasoning, and augment rather than replace human agency — designing the interaction layer between people and intelligent machines.

HCI Theory &
