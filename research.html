Research

Designing interactions.
Building understanding.

Designing, studying, and theorising interaction at the intersection of spatial computing, novel interactions, and intelligent systems.

Research Themes

What am I currently working on?

01

Distributed Mixed Reality Collaboration

How do people work together when their physical spaces are different but their digital environments are shared? Research on spatial heterogeneity, presence, and novel interaction primitives for distributed teams in blended physical–digital spaces.

View publications →
02

Human-Centred AI & Trustworthy Systems

When AI systems make or assist consequential decisions, what do people understand, trust, and contest? Research spanning explainability, algorithmic contestability, responsibility attribution, and the design of AI systems people can meaningfully engage with.

View publications →
03

Gaze, Multimodal Interaction & Input Techniques

Eyes move faster than hands. Research on smooth pursuit eye movements, attention-aware interfaces, gaze–speech fusion, and novel input techniques — from smart watches and VR headsets to handheld devices and large interactive surfaces.

View publications →
04

Embodied Learning, Movement & Performance

Learning happens in the body. Research on movement analysis, physical skill acquisition, and immersive environments designed to support athletes, dancers, students, and clinical practitioners — where feedback, timing, and physicality are everything.

View publications →
05

HCI Theory & Research Methodology

Good research needs good foundations. Work on statistical methods for data analysis and causal reasoning, measurement validity, research synthesis, and the epistemological frameworks that shape how HCI knowledge is produced and applied.

View publications →
